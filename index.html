I recently completed my M.Sc. in Machine Learning at UCL, working on multilingual NLP, reinforcement learning and large language models.

My goal is to democratize AI along the multilinguality and efficiency axes. My research spans how models process and think across different languages, how they can be made smaller and cheaper without losing what matters and how we keep them safe, aligned and interpretable along the way. My most recent paper got accepted to EMNLP 2026 Main.

I’ve studied and worked in Germany, the UK, China and Tunisia and I speak German, English, Mandarin, Arabic and French. These experiences shaped what I care about most about: building language technology that is accessible for everyone, in every language and under every computational budget.

My commitment to equitable and multilingual NLP helped me secure the highly competitive DAAD scholarship for my Master's in London, as well as a PhD offer from the University of Cambridge.
